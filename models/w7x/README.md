# W7-X quench models and validation data

OPENSC2 models of the Wendelstein 7-X non-planar coil conductor used in the
paper referenced below, together with the measurement and reference data the
simulations are compared with, and the scripts that run each model and draw
its figures. Simulation results and figures are not part of the package; they
are regenerated by the scripts.

Each model directory holds its OPENSC2 input deck (`simulation.yaml`,
`conductor_CONDUCTOR_*.yaml`, auxiliary CSV files), a `run_model.py` that
runs it and a `make_figures.py` that draws its figures from the results.

### The winding-pack (WP) models

Six conductors `CONDUCTOR_100` ... `CONDUCTOR_600` represent the six
hydraulic double layers of the coil (144.5-164.5 m each, 14000 elements
each). The magnetic field along every double layer (`external_bfield.csv`)
is the coil self-field at 17.6 kA and scales with the coil current. The
transverse heat conduction through the turn and layer insulation is
described by the patch tables `transverse_CONDUCTOR_*.csv`. The hydraulic
network in `simulation.yaml` is the documented CEA Saclay winding circuit:
supply reservoir, inlet filter, three pair trims feeding two double layers
each, per-DL inlet headers, four outlet headers (DL1 | DL2+DL3 | DL4+DL5 |
DL6), return manifold and return line.

The runs are driven by `common/controller.py`:

1. the supply reservoir follows the measured supply pressure PT2111 and
   temperature TT2113C; the simulation starts 1100 s before the measured
   quench on the 6.18 K plateau and sees the final staircase step to 6.28 K;
2. a quench is detected when the resistive voltage of any double layer
   reaches 0.5 V; 0.184 s later the coil current follows the measured dump
   (IAL-C1); from the detection on, the measured schedules are replayed
   anchored to the model detection;
3. the run stops 30 s after the dump starts (15 s in the no-network model)
   and writes `final_run_log.tsv` (double-layer voltages, outlet flows) and
   `hotspot_log.tsv` (hot spot of strand, jacket and epoxy per double layer).

`wp_model_no_induced_losses` switches every induced-loss source off (deck
keys zeroed and `--no-induced-losses` baked into its driver).
`wp_model_no_network` has no network section: every channel sees the
measured supply/return pressures and 6.1 K, no schedules are applied and the
quench is started by a heat impulse. `wp_model_no_transv` has the transverse
coupling tables removed.

### The adiabatic model

`adiabatic_model` is the single-conductor W7-X case of the THEA benchmark:
one double layer at 4.1 K and 15320 A, 17000 elements (14450 of them in
60-100 m), a 2 J heat impulse at 80.0-80.1 m, and the W7-X current dump
prescribed in `external_current.csv`. There is no controller; the deck runs
to 10 s.

## Requirements

OPENSC2 of this repository with its virtual environment (`.venv` in the
repository root: numpy, scipy, pandas, CoolProp, matplotlib, PyYAML, numba).
All commands below are given from the repository root.

## Running a model

```
.venv/bin/python models/w7x/wp_model_full/run_model.py
.venv/bin/python models/w7x/wp_model_no_induced_losses/run_model.py
.venv/bin/python models/w7x/wp_model_no_network/run_model.py
.venv/bin/python models/w7x/wp_model_no_transv/run_model.py
.venv/bin/python models/w7x/adiabatic_model/run_model.py
```

Every `run_model.py` carries the configuration of its model as defaults
(`--help` lists them). Results are written to
`<model>/simulation_results/<name>/Output/` (`W7X_QT` for the WP models,
`W7X` for the adiabatic model) plus the two controller logs in the model
directory. Wall-clock on one core of a 2024 workstation: about 3 h for
`wp_model_full`, `wp_model_no_induced_losses` and `wp_model_no_transv`
(most of it the 2400 s pre-quench settle), about 1.3 h for
`wp_model_no_network`, about 20 min for `adiabatic_model`.

For a quick functional test, `--mesh-elements N --end-time T` run a reduced
copy of the deck under `<model>/smoke_runs/` without touching the deck,
for example

```
.venv/bin/python models/w7x/wp_model_full/run_model.py --mesh-elements 300 --end-time 20
.venv/bin/python models/w7x/wp_model_no_network/run_model.py --mesh-elements 3000 --end-time 3 --post-discharge 1.5
.venv/bin/python models/w7x/adiabatic_model/run_model.py --mesh-elements 300 --end-time 1
```

(the network models cannot reach their quench in a short run; the
no-network model needs about 3000 elements per conductor for the 0.1 m heat
impulse to ignite, it then detects at about 0.13 s and dumps; the
`make_figures.py` scripts accept `--model-dir <model>/smoke_runs/<run>` to
draw from such a run).

## Figures

```
.venv/bin/python models/w7x/<model>/make_figures.py       # after run_model.py
.venv/bin/python models/w7x/make_combined_figures.py      # after all four WP runs
```

Per WP model, `figures/` receives the hot-spot time evolutions, the spatial
snapshots before and after the quench and the CEA outlet-temperature
comparison, and `publication-ready/` the publication set: detection voltage
(model double layers against DUP11-3), outlet temperatures of the four
groups against TT2100A-TT2103A and the 1D THEA run, group and double-layer
flows, strand and channel-pressure profiles 2 s after the quench, strand
hot spots. The no-network model draws the network-independent subset and the
channel outlet temperatures instead of the group temperatures.
`make_combined_figures.py` builds the 2 x 2 panels (full-feature, no induced
losses, no network, no transverse conduction) of the detection voltage, the
strand hot spots, the outlet temperatures of groups 2-4 and the strand
profiles into `models/w7x/publication-ready/`. The adiabatic model's
`make_figures.py` plots its hot spot against the THEA reference with the
relative difference.

All times in the WP figures are event times: the model detection is placed
at the measured detection, +0.461 s after the fast-acquisition trigger; the
current dump starts at +0.645 s.

## Validation data

`validation_data/aab51/` holds the subset of the AAB51 quench-test
measurements the package needs (see `PROVENANCE.md` and `readme.txt` there):

| sensor | quantity | role |
|---|---|---|
| PT2111, TT2113C | supply pressure (bar) and temperature (K) | drive the supply reservoir of the WP runs |
| IAL-C1 | coil current (A) | dump waveform applied at the model discharge |
| DUP11-3 | compensated voltage of layers (1,2)-(3,4) (V, clips at 1 V) | detection-voltage figures |
| TT2100A ... TT2103A | outlet temperatures of the four double-layer groups (K) | outlet-temperature figures |

`Quench/` holds the fast event records (window -1.43 ... +8.57 s around the
trigger, uppercase files 0.2 ms, lowercase 10 ms), `Slow/` the slow control
archive of the same sensors trimmed to -4500 ... +300 s. The files keep the
archive format (Latin-1 text, `dd/mm/yyyy hh:mm:ss.f value` rows in the slow
archive). `common/measurement_data.py` reads them.

`validation_data/thea/` holds the THEA results: the hot-spot histories of the
W7-X benchmark case (`high_fidelity_data_*.csv`, compared with
`adiabatic_model`) and the 1D THEA model of the AAB51 test
(`cea_data_*.csv`, the "adiabatic" trace of the WP outlet figures); see
`PROVENANCE.md` there.

## Layout of `common/`

| module | purpose |
|---|---|
| `constants.py` | event-time conventions (simulation start -1100 s, detection +0.461 s, dump delay 0.184 s) |
| `measurement_data.py` | readers of the fast and slow measurement files |
| `controller.py` | `QuenchTestController`: schedules, detection, discharge, logs |
| `run_wp_model.py` | command line of the drivers, reduced-deck smoke runs |
| `figure_library.py` | data access and standard figures of a WP run |
| `publication_figures.py` | publication figures of a WP run |
| `no_network_figures.py` | figure set of the run without network |
| `tools/` | one-off scripts that built `validation_data/aab51` and `adiabatic_model/external_current.csv` |
